How the score works. Each service: on time = 100 points. Late = 100 minus 2 points for every minute late (so 10 minutes late = 80, 50 minutes or more = 0). Absent = 0. Excused services are not counted. A worker's score is the average over their services. Ties go to the worker who arrives earlier on average.
